About the Research & Project

An innovative digital learning solution developed to bridge practical tour guiding competencies and interactive web technology for vocational tourism students.

Project Overview

This e-module is developed as an undergraduate thesis project focusing on enhancing tour guiding competence—specifically in giving directions and explaining safety procedures. By integrating interactive simulation models and real-time browser-based speech evaluation, learners can practice independently and master professional English expressions used in the tourism industry.

Target Learner

Vocational Tourism Students & Tour Guiding Trainees.

Methodology

ADDE Instructional Design Model (Analysis, Design, Development, Evaluation).

Technology Stack

HTML5, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript, and Web Speech API.
