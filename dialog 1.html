Tourist
Excuse me, how do I get to Gitgit Waterfall from here?
Tour Guide
Sure! Follow this pathway straight ahead, and you'll pass a small row of souvenir stalls on your left.
Tourist
Okay, I see the stalls. What's next?
Tour Guide
Keep going until you reach a wooden signpost pointing downhill, that's where the trail to the waterfall begins.
Tourist
Is the trail easy to walk on?
Tour Guide
It's mostly stone steps, so just take your time, especially if it rained recently.
Tourist
Is it far from the parking area?
Tour Guide
Not too far, it's about a 10-minute walk. Just follow the signpost, you can't miss it.
Tourist
Will I hear the waterfall before I see it?
Tour Guide
Yes, actually! Once you start hearing the water, you're almost there.
Tourist
Great, thank you so much!
Tour Guide
You're welcome! Enjoy the waterfall, and watch your step on the way down.
Tourist
Hi, could you tell me how to get to Pasar Anyar from here?
Tour Guide
Of course. Go straight along the main road until you reach the intersection.
Tourist
Then what should I do?
Tour Guide
Turn right at the intersection, and the market will be on your left, just across from the old clock tower.
Tourist
Is the clock tower a big landmark? I want to make sure I don't miss it.
Tour Guide
Yes, it's quite tall and hard to miss, it's one of the oldest landmarks in Singaraja.
Tourist
Good to know. How long does it take on foot?
Tour Guide
About 15 minutes, or you can take a short bike ride if you'd prefer.
Tourist
Is the market open all day?
Tour Guide
It opens early in the morning and gets quieter by early afternoon, so morning is the best time to visit.
Tourist
Perfect, I'll head there now. Thanks a lot!
Tour Guide
No problem, have a great time exploring and try the local snacks near the entrance!
Tourist
Excuse me, I'm looking for the dolphin watching meeting point. Can you help?
Tour Guide
Sure! Go straight down this street until you reach the roundabout.
Tourist
Okay, and after the roundabout?
Tour Guide
Take the second exit, then it's just around the corner, you'll see the boats lined up on the beach.
Tourist
Is it close to the hotel area?
Tour Guide
Yes, it's right next to the row of cafés, about 5 minutes on foot.
Tourist
Perfect, does that mean I don't need transport?
Tour Guide
Exactly, walking is enough, especially this early in the morning.
Tourist
Got it. Is there anywhere to buy breakfast nearby before I go?
Tour Guide
Yes, there's a small warung right beside the meeting point that opens early, perfect for a quick bite.
Tourist
That's great, thank you for all the details!
Tour Guide
You're welcome! Enjoy the dolphin watching, it's a beautiful way to start the morning.
