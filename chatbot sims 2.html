Unit 2 Safety Briefing Guidelines

1. Gitgit Waterfall Trek

Key Rules: Slippery surfaces

Stay on marked trail.
Do not step on wet rocks.
Inform guide if weather changes (heavy rain).

2. Lovina Beach Activities

Key Rules: Water safety

Adult supervision required.
Wear life jackets for boats.
Swim only in designated areas.

3. Buleleng Nature Trail

Key Rules: Group & Pace

Walk at comfortable pace.
Hold handrails on steps.
Rest at designated rest stops.

Rubrik Penilaian D.2 Evaluation Criteria

Rubrik Penilaian D.2 Overview

Your performance is scored based on 4 key aspects. Each aspect is graded from score 1 (Needs Guidance) to 4 (Excellent). Final Score = (Total Score ÷ 16) × 100.

1. Ketepatan Instruksi Keselamatan (Safety Instruction Accuracy)

Score 4: Instruksi keselamatan sangat akurat, tegas, dan disertai alasan logis.
Score 3: Instruksi akurat, penjelasan alasan minim.
Score 2: Instruksi cukup jelas, ada sedikit keraguan.
Score 1: Instruksi tidak akurat atau membahayakan.

2. Penggunaan Ekspresi (Expression & Modals)

Score 4: Menggunakan modal verbs (must/should/please) dengan variatif & sopan.
Score 3: Sesuai konteks, kurang variatif.
Score 2: Ekspresi terbatas/berulang.
Score 1: Ekspresi tidak sesuai konteks.

3. Pelafalan & Kebahasaan (Pronunciation & Grammar)

Score 4: Jelas, alami, & menenangkan.
Score 3: Jelas, sedikit kesalahan.
Score 2: Dapat dipahami, banyak kesalahan tata bahasa.
Score 1: Sulit dipahami.

4. Interaksi & Kelancaran (Interaction & Responsiveness)

Score 4: Tanggap terhadap pertanyaan/tantangan turis & sangat lancar.
Score 3: Responsif, sedikit jeda.
Score 2: Interaksi terbata-bata.
Score 1: Interaksi sangat terbatas.
