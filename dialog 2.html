Home Dashboard Unit 1: Giving Directions Unit 2: Safety Procedures
Tour Guide
Good morning, everyone. Before we start our beach activity, I'd like to give you a short safety briefing.
Tourist
Sure. What should we know?
Tour Guide
First, please stay within the designated swimming area and follow the safety signs.
Tourist
Are there any areas we should avoid?
Tour Guide
Yes. Please do not swim beyond the marked area. The sea conditions can change quickly.
Tourist
What should we do if we see a warning flag?
Tour Guide
Please pay attention to the warning flags. If you see a red flag, do not enter the water.
Tourist
What if we have an emergency?
Tour Guide
If you need help, stay calm and contact the lifeguard immediately. The first-aid kit is available at the beach information center.
Tourist
Okay. So we should stay in the designated area and follow the safety signs.
Tour Guide
Exactly. Please stay together, follow the instructions, and let me know if you need any assistance.
Tour Guide
Before we start the walk, please listen to these safety instructions.
Tourist
Okay. Is the trail difficult?
Tour Guide
Some parts of the trail can be slippery, especially after rain.
Tourist
What should we do?
Tour Guide
Please walk carefully and stay on the marked trail. Do not walk outside the designated pathway.
Tourist
Can we take pictures near the waterfall?
Tour Guide
Yes, but please be careful. Do not stand too close to the edge or climb on wet rocks.
Tourist
What should we do if someone falls?
Tour Guide
Do not panic. Stay with the group and immediately inform me or the local staff.
Tourist
Got it. We should stay on the marked trail and be careful around the rocks.
Tour Guide
That's right. Safety comes first. Please follow the instructions throughout the trip.
Tourist
Before we enter the temple, I'd like to explain some important rules and safety procedures.
Tour Guide
Are there any special rules we need to follow?
Tourist
Yes. Please wear a sarong and sash when entering the temple area.
Tour Guide
Can we take photographs inside?
Tourist
Photography is allowed in some areas, but please follow the signs and do not take photographs in restricted areas.
Tour Guide
What else should we be careful about?
Tourist
Please respect the ceremony if one is taking place. Keep your voice low and do not disturb people who are praying.
Tour Guide
Can we enter every area of the temple?
Tourist
No. Please do not enter restricted areas. Always follow the signs and instructions from the temple staff.
Tour Guide
Understood.
Tourist
Thank you for respecting the local rules and culture. If you are unsure about something, please ask me before doing it.
